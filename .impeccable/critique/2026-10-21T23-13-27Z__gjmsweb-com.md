## Design specificity — high (9/10), preserve the world

Domain-shaped objects, not decoration: the article row as an editorial entry, year→volume→issue grouping with a sticky year rail, the ISSN/volume/pages record panel and copy-ready citation card. ## Resolved in this pass
All five P1s and all eight P2s, plus: guidelines contents rail now lists "Before you submit" and "Disclaimer" and marks the current section (aria-current); discrete filter changes push a history entry so Back undoes a filter instead of leaving the page (articles and archive); a noscript note points JavaScript-less visitors at the issue pages; the board no longer says "Showing 23 of 23"; widows removed from list items and definition values; footer "Publication fee" deep-links to #key-facts like the home page.

## Deliberate deviations (not defects)
- The eyebrow/kicker above the page H1 is the PageHeader rhythm, one per page, never above every section heading; the same token doubles as the group-label style in asides. Documenting that is a DESIGN.md job, not a silent restyle of 19 call sites.
- Archive and Articles expose different filter sets on purpose: one answers "which issue", the other "which paper". Unifying them adds controls to both.
- --band-muted on the navy band measures 7.08:1; the detector's gray-on-color rule is an aesthetic heuristic, not a contrast failure.
