About the Journal

The Global Journal of Management, Social Sciences and Humanities is a peer-reviewed academic platform committed to advancing knowledge across multiple disciplines. Since 2015, we have published over 500 articles from researchers worldwide, maintaining the highest standards of scholarly rigor and ethical publication practices.

Our blind peer-review process ensures objective evaluation of every manuscript, while our international editorial board brings diverse perspectives to academic discourse. We believe in open access to knowledge, making research freely available to the global academic community.

Serving as a bridge between theory and practice, GJMSSH publishes original research, theoretical frameworks, and applied studies that contribute meaningfully to management, social sciences, and humanities disciplines.
