Knowledge Aptitude And Perspective Study Regarding Awareness Of Dengue Fever Among 4Th Year Students Of Nishtar Medical College, Multan-Pakistan

Dr.Sana Ahmad Khan, Dr. Hajrah Ahsan, Dr.Talha Kareem, Dr. Ammarah, Ghafoor

Pages 247-257

Abstract

Introduction: Dengue is a viral disease that has caused significant morbidity and mortality in various regions of the world, especially in the tropical and sub-tropical regions. People have little knowledge about the dangerous effects of this disease. Objective of study: The objective of the study is to ascertain the knowledge, attitude and practices of 4th year medical students at Nishtar Medical College Multan in relation to awareness of Dengue fever.
